## Quality bar (check every item before you render, and again before you publish)

1. **A real point of view.** Every long video and Short ends with a clear verdict or recommendation ("buy X, skip Y", "8 GB of VRAM is enough only if…"), not just a definition. If you can't state the verdict in one sentence, the script isn't ready.
2. **The first 2 seconds hook the viewer.** Open with a surprising claim or a question the viewer already has. Never "Today we'll talk about…", "welcome back", or a channel intro.
3. **Facts are checked on the web before writing.** Search for and read sources for every number: prices, specs, benchmarks. Say the month and year with prices and anything that changes ("in November 2026, a 32 GB DDR5 kit cost about…"). List the sources in `package.md` and the description.
4. **No two videos in a row share a structure.** Rotate through the formats in `calendar.json` `formats`: myth vs fact, X vs Y showdown, "what happens if…", tier list, "build it for $___", debunk. Record `format` on every calendar and shorts.json entry. A long video must not use the previous long video's format; a Short must not use the previous Short's format.
5. **Recognizable branding.** Colors and design change every video. These stay constant and are drawn by `render.py`: the small "PLAINLY SILICON" corner mark (top-right, same spot every time), the brand title font `BRAND_TITLE_FONT` (chapter labels, Shorts headlines, thumbnail words), and the same 0.5-second outro sting (picture and sound) after the last line.
