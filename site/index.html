About the Plainly Silicon Uploader

The Plainly Silicon Uploader is a small private tool used only by the owner of the Plainly Silicon channel. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload the channel's own finished videos, with their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails, to the Plainly Silicon YouTube channel on a schedule.

  • It only ever acts on the owner's own channel, after the owner signs in with Google and grants access.
  • It requests one permission: uploading videos to that channel (youtube.upload).
  • It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not read, collect or share anyone else's data.

Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. The tool uses YouTube API Services, so the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy also apply.
