- TechSpot, "Ultra vs. High Settings in PC Games" (Ultra to High typically 15 to 25 percent; Cyberpunk 2077 24 percent; Microsoft Flight Simulator 4K 45 to 57 FPS): https://www.techspot.com/article/2338-ultra-vs-high-settings/
