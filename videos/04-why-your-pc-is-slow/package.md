# Video 4: Why Your PC Is Slow — and Why More RAM Usually Isn't the Fix

Length: 7:28 · Theme: circuit · Voice: Kokoro am_michael · Narration: `narration.txt`

## Hook and structure

One slow five-year-old desktop (8 GB RAM, hard drive) that takes two minutes to become usable, and the internet's advice to "buy more RAM". We diagnose it reading by reading with Task Manager: the Processes columns and sorting, the bottleneck idea, what 100% disk (active time) really means, why a hard drive's seek + spin time makes requests queue, startup apps and their impact ratings, how a RAM shortage spills into the page file and also shows up as disk activity, how to tell the two apart with In use and Committed, when more RAM does help, CPU hogs and thermal throttling, the GPU column for gamers, and the verdict: the drive, not the RAM. Visuals are square-cornered instrument windows, vertical meters, sparklines and request tokens queuing at a slow drive.

## Description

Your PC is slow and everyone says "buy more RAM", but one free tool already on your PC can show you the real bottleneck first. We diagnose one slow desktop step by step with Task Manager: what 100% disk actually means, why hard drives choke, startup apps, the page file, how to tell if you're really short on RAM, CPU hogs, thermal throttling and the GPU column.
