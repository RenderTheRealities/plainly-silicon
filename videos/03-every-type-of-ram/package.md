## Hook and structure

One old DDR3 stick and one question, "will it fit my new motherboard?", carried through every RAM generation and form factor and answered at the end. Visuals are front-view module diagrams: contacts counted, notches compared, a curved DDR4 edge seating into a slot, a DDR3 stick landing on a DDR4 slot's ridge, DDR4/DDR5 sticks overlaid, one 64-bit road vs two 32-bit roads, PMIC power flow, an on-die ECC bit flip, SO-DIMMs to scale, soldered LPDDR, GDDR around a GPU.

## Title

Every Type of RAM Explained: DDR3 to DDR5

## Description

Will an old stick of RAM fit your new motherboard, and would it even work? We follow one DDR3 stick through DDR4 and DDR5, then into laptops (SO-DIMM), thin laptops (soldered LPDDR) and graphics cards (GDDR), so you can tell any memory apart and know why the generations can't be swapped.
