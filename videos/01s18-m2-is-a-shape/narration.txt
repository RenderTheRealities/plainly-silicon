Two M dot 2 drives can look identical, yet one can be ten times faster.

That's because M dot 2 is a shape, not a speed. It's a small stick that screws flat onto your motherboard.

Some use SATA, the same link as a two and a half inch SSD. They top out around 550 megabytes per second.

Others use N V M e over P C I e lanes. Even older ones reach about 3,500 megabytes per second, and newer drives pass 7,000.

To tell them apart, check the notches. SATA sticks usually have two, B and M. N V M e sticks usually have one, the M key. The label tells you for sure.
