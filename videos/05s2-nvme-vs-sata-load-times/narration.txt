Your N V M e drive is about thirteen times faster than a Sata S S D. So why don't your games load any faster?

Sata tops out around 550 megabytes per second. A P C I e four drive, like Samsung's 990 Pro, is rated at 7,450.

Yet in TechSpot's tests, a Cyberpunk save reloaded in about the same time on every S S D, Sata included. Starfield loaded less than a second apart on P C I e five and three.

The drive isn't the slow part anymore. Your processor still has to unpack the files and build the level.

Games using Direct Storage hand that unpacking to the graphics card. For most others, any S S D is enough.
