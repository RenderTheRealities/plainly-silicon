Task Manager says Disk, 100 percent. Your drive isn't full, so what's maxed out?

That number is active time. It's how much of the time your drive was busy. It says nothing about free space.

A hard drive reads one scattered spot at a time. Its arm swings to the right track, then waits for the platter to spin the data underneath. That's roughly 13 milliseconds per read.

It manages only about 75 to 100 random reads per second. When startup apps all want files at once, the line never empties. Busy every moment means 100 percent.

An SSD has no arm. It answers in well under a millisecond, so the same work barely registers.
