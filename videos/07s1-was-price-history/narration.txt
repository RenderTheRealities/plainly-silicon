That crossed-out "was" price on a Black Friday deal may be the least honest number on the page.

A graphics card that says "was 399, now 299" feels like a hundred dollars off.

Last year, the consumer group Which tracked 175 sale products from eight big retailers. Eighty-three percent were the same price or cheaper at least once outside the sale.

Not one was at its cheapest only on Black Friday.

So ignore the sticker. Check a price history tracker, like PCPartPicker or camelcamelcamel. If it's been 299 since summer, you saved nothing.
