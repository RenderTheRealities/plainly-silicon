Here's every part inside your PC, and what it actually does, in under a minute.

The power supply turns wall power into clean, low-voltage power. The motherboard connects everything, like the roads of a city.

The CPU is the brain. It runs billions of tiny instructions every second. The GPU is a crowd of thousands of small cores that draws every frame you see.

Storage is long-term memory. It keeps your files when the power is off. RAM is short-term memory. It's fast, but it forgets everything when you shut down.
