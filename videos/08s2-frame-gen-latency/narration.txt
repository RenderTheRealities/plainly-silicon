Frame generation can double your FPS counter. So why does the game feel the same?

The GPU renders two real frames, then builds a predicted frame that goes between them. Your screen gets twice the pictures.

But only real frames come from the game, and that's where your mouse gets read. The inserted frame is a guess, so it can't react to anything you just did.

60 real frames shown as 120 still respond like 60. Building that middle frame also means holding back the newest real one, adding a little delay. That's why NVIDIA pairs it with Reflex.

AMD recommends at least 60 real frames per second first, and says avoid it below 30.

It's smoothness, not speed. The real fixes are in our video, What Actually Raises FPS.
