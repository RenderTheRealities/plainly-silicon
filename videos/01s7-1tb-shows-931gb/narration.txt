You bought a one terabyte drive. Windows says it's 931 gigabytes. Where did 69 gigabytes go?

Nowhere. Nothing is missing. It's two different ways of counting.

Drive makers count in thousands. To them, one terabyte is exactly one trillion bytes.

But Windows counts in powers of two. To Windows, a gigabyte is 1,024 times 1,024 times 1,024 bytes, which is a bit more than a billion. Divide one trillion bytes by that bigger number, and you get about 931.
