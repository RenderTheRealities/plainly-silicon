Why do processor reviews test games at 1080p on the fastest graphics card they can get? It's on purpose.

Each frame, the processor goes first, then the graphics card draws it. The slower one sets your frame rate.

With an RTX 3060, TechSpot found a Core i9 just 3 percent faster than a budget Core i3. The graphics card was the wall, so both processors waited.

Swap in an RTX 4090 and the gap jumps to 82 percent. That's 186 frames per second against 102.

Low resolution on a huge graphics card gets the GPU out of the way. What's left is the processor's real ceiling, the one you'll hit after your next upgrade.
