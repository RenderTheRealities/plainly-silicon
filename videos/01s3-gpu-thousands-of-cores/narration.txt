Your CPU might have 8 cores. Your graphics card has thousands. So why isn't the GPU in charge?

CPU cores are like a few expert workers. Each one is fast and clever, and great at complicated, step-by-step jobs.

GPU cores are a huge crowd of simpler workers. On their own, they're not that smart.

But a frame is millions of pixels, and most of them can be worked out at the same time. Give each worker a few pixels, and the whole picture fills in at once.

That's why games lean on the GPU for frame rate, while the CPU runs everything else. See every part of your PC explained in the full video on the channel.
