Through-line: follow one example gaming PC's 400 watts from the wall outlet through every stage of the power supply (filter, bridge rectifier, active PFC, bulk capacitor, high-frequency switching, transformer, secondary rectification and smoothing, feedback) to steady 12 volts at the graphics card. Then use the same 400 watts to explain 80 Plus (Gold pulls about 444 W, basic 80 Plus about 500 W), what the label wattage means and how to size a unit, GPU power spikes and ATX 3.0 excursions, why cheap units fail (capacitor life vs temperature) and the protection circuits that matter. Visuals: an electronics bench. Oscilloscope screens with graticules show the waveform at each stage, energy moves as square packets, efficiency is a Sankey split with rising heat, load is shown on analog dials, protections are breaker switches.
