One menu change can give you almost a quarter more frames per second, and you'll barely see it.

TechSpot tested Cyberpunk 2077 on both presets. Dropping from Ultra to High gave twenty four percent more frames. Side by side, the pictures looked almost the same.

So what does Ultra buy you? In the current version, High mostly trims reflections, fog and crowd size. You'd spot that in a paused screenshot, not mid fight.

The gain varies. On that newer version, TechSpot measured fourteen percent. Games whose presets differ in only a few settings barely change.

So start from High, then raise textures back up if your graphics card has the memory.
