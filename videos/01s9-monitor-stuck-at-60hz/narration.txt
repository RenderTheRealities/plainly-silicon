You paid for a 144 hertz monitor. There's a real chance it's still running at 60.

Refresh rate is how many times per second your screen draws a new image. A higher number means smoother motion, especially in games.

But Windows doesn't always switch to the highest rate on its own. Many high refresh monitors start out at 60 hertz until you change it.

On Windows 11, open Settings, then System, Display, Advanced display, and choose a refresh rate. If the higher option is missing, your cable or port might be the limit, so try the DisplayPort cable that came with the monitor.
