If you have a graphics card, check which port your monitor is plugged into. A lot of people get this wrong.

Your PC has two sets of video ports. One set is on the motherboard, near the USB ports. The other is lower down, on the graphics card itself.

The motherboard ports come from the processor's built-in graphics, not your graphics card. And if your processor has no built-in graphics, those ports may not work at all.

Plug into the motherboard, and your expensive graphics card can end up sitting idle while games run on much weaker built-in graphics.

So follow the cable. It should go into the graphics card, lower down on the back of the case. More PC hardware explained on the channel.
