# Resizable BAR Off? Your CPU Sees Only 256 MB of VRAM #shorts

## Description
Full video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=15pV5fzsfSU
Without Resizable BAR, your CPU reaches your graphics card's memory through a 256 MB window. Intel Arc cards are built around having it switched on: with it off, TechPowerUp measured an Arc A770 at about 77% of its normal 1080p performance.
