Your graphics card might have 12 gigabytes of memory. Without one setting, your processor reaches just 256 megabytes of it at a time.

That window is a leftover from 32-bit computers. To reach the rest, the CPU keeps sliding it along.

Resizable BAR opens the window to the whole card. Textures and game data go straight in.

Nvidia and AMD cards gain a little. Intel Arc cards are built around it. With it off, TechPowerUp's Arc A770 ran almost a quarter slower at 1080p.

So check your BIOS before buying Arc. If your board can't turn it on, skip Arc.
