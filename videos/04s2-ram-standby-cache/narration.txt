Open Resource Monitor, and you might see almost no free memory. Don't panic. That's usually on purpose.

Windows fills spare RAM with files and apps you've used, in case you need them. A service called SysMain learns your habits and loads things early. That's the standby cache.

Task Manager lists it as Cached. It isn't counted as In use, and it still counts as available.

When an app needs room, Windows drops cached data and hands that memory over. Empty RAM does nothing. Cached RAM can make the next launch faster.

Check two numbers on the Performance tab. If In use sits near your total and Committed keeps climbing toward its limit, you're really short on memory.
