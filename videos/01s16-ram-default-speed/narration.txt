That 6000 speed on your RAM box? Your PC probably isn't using it.

New memory starts at a safe default speed set by an industry standard. For D D R 5, that's usually 4800. For D D R 4, it's often 2133.

The faster number on the box is a profile stored on the stick itself. Intel calls it X M P. AMD calls it EXPO. It's a tested overclock, and your PC won't use it until you turn it on.

Open your BIOS, find the X M P or EXPO setting, pick the profile, and save. Then check the memory speed in Task Manager.
