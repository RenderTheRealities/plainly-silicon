RAM and storage are both memory. So why does your PC need both?

Think of storage as a filing cabinet. It's huge, and it keeps everything, even with the power off. But walking over to it takes time.

RAM is your desk. It's small, but everything on it is right in front of you, so the CPU can grab it in nanoseconds.

When you open a game, its files move from the cabinet onto the desk. When you shut down, the desk is wiped clean. The cabinet keeps everything.

That's why more RAM only helps if your desk is too small. For most gaming, 16 to 32 gigabytes is plenty. The full breakdown of every part is on the channel.
