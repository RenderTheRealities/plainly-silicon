Two 8 gigabyte sticks of RAM can be faster than one 16 gigabyte stick. Same total memory, so why?

Your processor talks to memory through channels, like lanes on a road. Most desktop processors have two of them.

With a single stick, only one lane is in use. Install two matching sticks in the right slots, and both lanes carry data at the same time.

That roughly doubles the peak bandwidth between the processor and memory. Games and built-in graphics often feel the difference most.

Check your motherboard manual for which slots to use. It's often the second and fourth. More PC hardware explained on the channel.
