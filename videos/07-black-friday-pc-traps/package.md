## Description

A Black Friday cart that says you saved $420 might have saved you almost nothing. We push one example cart through seven traps: inflated "was" prices, same-name GPUs with different chips, silent SSD part swaps, laptop GPU power limits, prebuilts with mystery parts, countdown timers and marketplace sellers, then turn it into a one-minute check you can use on any PC deal.
