GTA 6 is finally out, and even a PS5 Pro can't run it at 60 frames a second.

Digital Foundry measured a 30 frame cap on both PlayStation 5 models, around 1440p, with no performance mode.

The graphics chip isn't the problem. Every pedestrian and car runs on the processor: eight Zen 2 cores from 2020.

The PS5 Pro's GPU is much bigger, but its processor is only about ten percent faster. So it just holds 30 more steadily.

PC isn't announced. One report says early 2027, and Digital Foundry warns even double the console's speed may miss 60 in crowds.
