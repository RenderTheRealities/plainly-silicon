Task Manager says your processor is at 40 percent. It can still be the part holding your frame rate back.

That number is an average across every thread your processor has. Games rarely spread their work evenly.

A lot of each frame waits on one main thread. On a 16 thread chip, one thread running flat out adds only about 6 percent to the total.

So that one busy thread sets the pace, while the others sit mostly idle and pull the average down.

Check GPU usage instead. If it's pinned at 95 percent or more, the graphics card is the limit. If it sits well below 90, with no frame cap or V-Sync, the processor is likely the wall.
